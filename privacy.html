Privacy

Privacy

UAP Idaho is a static information site: no accounts, no forms that send data, no analytics, no ads, no cookies of our own. We do not sell personal information and we do not publish raw case files.

What happens today

  • Hosting logs. The site is served by a third-party static host. Like any web host, it may log your IP address, browser and the pages requested to deliver the site and keep it secure. We have no visitor list.
  • Email you send us. If you write to uapmontana@pm.me we keep the thread so we can answer and, for reports, investigate.

Everything else

UAP Idaho follows the same privacy practices as UAP Montana, including how sighting reports will be handled once a secure form exists: never published, stored in a separate secure database. See the full UAP Montana privacy page. Anything new we add here will be written up on this page before it goes live.

Questions: uapmontana@pm.me. Last updated 2026-10-02.
