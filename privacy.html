Privacy

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no cookies of its own and no forms that send data.

What the host sees

The pages are delivered by a third-party static host. Like any web host, it may record your IP address, browser type and the pages you request in order to serve and protect the site. We do not receive a visitor list.

If you write to us

When a contact address is published, messages sent to it are kept so we can reply and, for sighting reports, follow up. We do not sell or share them, and report details and contact information are never posted publicly. To ask us to delete something you sent, write to us and we will remove it unless the law requires us to keep it.

Changes

If we add anything that collects data, we will describe it on this page before it goes live.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
