About UAP Idaho

The Idaho satellite of UAP Montana: an independent, public-source field-notes project working from evidence it can show.

Relationship to UAP Montana

UAP Montana is the primary brand. UAP Idaho shares its standards, its design and its contact inbox, and keeps a thinner site. Read About UAP Montana for how we work.

What we will cite

Public X and news, AMS/IMO fireball notices, space weather, FAA GPS-test windows, and historical NUFORC patterns. If a source is down, we say it’s down.

Independence

This site is independent, is not affiliated with any government agency, and does not publish confidential case files or witness details.

Reports

UFO / UAP reports are encouraged and all reports are investigated. There is no Idaho form; email uapmontana@pm.me. A secure database will come first before any form goes live — see Contact.

What never goes up. Invented sightings. Witness personal details. Case-system dumps. “We have classified files” theater. A separate Idaho case feed.
